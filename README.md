# pwc-technology-consulting-simulation
# [Client Type] - ERP Upgrade & SOX Compliance Engagement
*PwC Technology Consulting Job Simulation (Forage)*

## Situation
An international manufacturing company (building materials & plumbing 
equipment) needed to consolidate four regional ERP systems into a single 
global platform, while addressing gaps in user access controls and SOX 
compliance.

## Complication
- Fragmented systems across 4 regions with inconsistent processes
- No standardized segregation of duties (SoD) controls
- Manual SOX controls testing creating compliance risk and inefficiency

## My Role
Associate on the Enterprise Technology Solutions (ETS) team, responsible for 
early-stage discovery and planning deliverables ahead of solution design.

## Approach & Methods
| Deliverable | Method / Framework Used |
|---|---|
| Client discovery interview guide | Structured stakeholder interviewing, mapped to project scope pillars (Security, Controls, Transformation) |
| Business requirements | Agile user stories, Gherkin (Given/When/Then) acceptance criteria |
| Delivery roadmap | Gantt-style MVP roadmap (Plan–Design–Build–Test–Train–Release–Post-Release) |
| Executive summary | Client-ready PowerPoint synthesis (Highlights, Goals, Scope, Risks, Roadmap) |
